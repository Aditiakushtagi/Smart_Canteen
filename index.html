SmartCanteen

Prepare Smarter. Waste Less. Feed More.

An intelligent food waste management system for college canteens. Record what is cooked and served, see where food is wasted, plan tomorrow's quantities and send safe surplus to people who need it.

Open Dashboard

The problem

A canteen cooks for a crowd it cannot predict. Cooking too much means food is thrown away, along with the water, energy and money used to make it. Cooking too little means students go without. Most canteens do not record what is left over, so the same mistakes repeat every day.

What SmartCanteen does

Track Waste

Daily entries of food prepared, served and left over, with waste percentage worked out automatically.

Predict Demand

A simple demo forecast of tomorrow's students and the quantity to cook, with the working shown.

Manage Surplus

Mark leftover food as available, donated, composted or disposed, and see a redistribution summary.

Measure Impact

Estimates of food, water and CO2 saved, plus a sustainability score and automatic insights.
